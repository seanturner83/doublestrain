## Findings

- **Pre-pass v1 let Jev make a confident false not-reachable.** On `adv-lodash-computed-property`
  (`_[\`${KIND}late\`](...)`) Jev answered not_reachable@0.98, clearing the 0.9 floor and silently
  demoting Act to Attend. The grep summary showed zero symbol mentions, so Jev was confident on
  an incomplete picture. A floor cannot catch that. Pre-pass v2 marks the summary
  **open-world** when the dependency is reached dynamically, and summary-only judges may not say
  not_reachable on an open-world summary. After the change: unsure, then escalate, then claude
  answers reachable.
- **Jev's raw "not reachable" below about 0.9 is unreliable.** It leaned not_reachable on axios
  (0.79) and on the renamed destructure (0.14), and both are really reachable. The asymmetric floor
  rejected both. Keep the 0.9 floor.
- **Local inference (27B, 2-bit, on the Mac) is a strong middle tier.** It resolved 18/22 with no
  errors and answered unsure exactly on the cases that need whole-tree tracing (config-driven
  `require`, transitive via express) or are ambiguous (qs comma, 0.85 against a 0.9 floor). It
  costs about 14s per call.
- **The three-tier cascade gets claude's accuracy at a quarter of its calls.** Jev answers 13
  candidates in about 0.3s each, local answers 5, and claude answers 3.
- **The one unresolved candidate is a labelling question, not a judge error:** qs CVE-2026-2391
  (needs `comma: true`) in a *library*. Every judge leans not_reachable, and claude (0.65) notes
  that a downstream consumer could opt in through `qsParseOptions`. It stays UNAVAILABLE, so Act
  rather than Attend: over-escalation, which is the safe direction.
